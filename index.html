QUESTIONS?

How do tickets work?

Tickets are sold per-city show. Grab yours early — spots are limited and shows do sell out. All tickets are digital.

How do performers get booked?

Submit an application using the form below. Our team reviews every submission and reaches out if it's a fit. We also scout talent directly.

Age requirements / accessibility?

Age requirements vary by venue. Accessibility information is included on each show's ticket page. Email us at info@nxtwave.live for specific accommodations.
