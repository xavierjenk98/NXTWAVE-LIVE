FAQ

Tickets are sold directly through our site. After purchase, you'll receive a confirmation email with your QR code — just show it at the door. Tickets are non-refundable but fully transferable. Doors typically open 30 minutes before showtime.

Artists and comedians can apply directly through the Apply section below. Our team reviews every submission and scouts independently at shows and online. If you're a fit for an upcoming stop, we'll reach out within 2 weeks of your application.

Age requirements vary by venue. Each show listing will note the age policy clearly — some are all-ages, others are 18+ or 21+. All venues we work with are ADA accessible. If you have specific accessibility needs, email us before the show and we'll make sure you're taken care of.
